---
title: Architecture decisions
description: Stable decisions that constrain the current platform rather than implementation history or future proposals.
---

Decision records explain why a durable constraint exists. They are concise and do not duplicate
runbooks or component documentation.

| Decision                                                                        | Status   | Consequence                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [ADR-0001: Capability-oriented monorepo](./0001-capability-monorepo)            | Accepted | One repository with explicit ownership and independent deployments |
| [ADR-0002: Contract-driven lakehouse catalog](./0002-contract-driven-lakehouse) | Accepted | YAML and PyIceberg own landing and curated metadata                |

New records use a monotonically increasing number and capture context, decision, consequences, and
rejected alternatives. Operational instructions belong elsewhere.
